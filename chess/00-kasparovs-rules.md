### Kasparov's 9 Rules

1. Never play an opening you don't understand.
	- Do not memorize openings.
2. You can't attack through a closed door, open the position.
	- Before attacking, make sure that you have follow ups.
	- Open positions by:
		- Pawn breaks
		- Sacrifice a piece
3. Do not attack until every piece is ready.
4. If you make 10 threats in a row, your opponent will blunder eventually.
5. When you can't attack, wait and improve.
6. Shift your focus to where the opponent has less pieces.
7. Never play hope chess.
8. Evaluation wins over calculation.
9. Your own games are your best study material.

### Summary
Plan the opening and follow-ups. Do not attack unless you have backup. Be patient. Evaluate instead of calculting.
Basically: Plan, Be patient and evaluate.
