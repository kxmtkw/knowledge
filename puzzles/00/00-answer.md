### Answer

First, think of what a balance really tells you? For a given object compared against a standard:

- It might tell you whether it is light.
- It might tell you whether it is heavy.
- It might tell you whether it is equal to the standard.

So there are `3 possibilities`. Since at any given time, only one possibility might be true, it means that 2 possibilities can be false.

So you must `divide the 8 coins into 3 groups of 3, 3, 2 coins`.

Here is how you can determine the fake coin by using the balance only two times.

- Put the the 3,3 stacks on the balance and compare.
- If both of them are equal, that means the third stack has the fake coin.
	- In that case, compare each of the coins in 2-coin stack, you will find your fake coin easily.
- If both of them are not equal, the fake coin is in the lighter one.
	- Take 2 coins from that lighter 3-coin stack and compare.
	- If both equal, then the third is the fake coint.
	- If both not equal, then the lighter one is the fake one.
