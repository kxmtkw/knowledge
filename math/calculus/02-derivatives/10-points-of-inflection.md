# Points of Inflection

> A point of inflection is a point on the curve of a function where the concavity of the graph changes.

## How to Find

1. Find the concavity intervals.
2. If across any boundary point (that is in the domain of $f$), the concavity (or the sign of the second derivative) changes, that point is a point of inflection.

Follow the model: `CSC` Concavity intervals, Sign Change
