# Critical Points

> A critical point of a function $f(x)$ is a point $c$ in the domain of $f$ where $f'(c)$ is either zero undefined.

-> `Points where the first derivative is zero or undefined`

> [!info] Significance
> Critical points are basically the points where a function *might* change behaviour.
> These points often mark the boundary points for *monotonicity* and are also candidates (along with endpoints in case of closed domain) for local and absolute extrema.

## How to Find

1. Find the first derivative of the function.
2. Find the values of $x$ at which the first derivative becomes equal to zero.
3. Find the values of $x$ at which the first derivative is undefined.
4. Check whether the points lie in the domain of the function.

Follow the model: `FZUC` First derivative, Zero, Undefined, Check
