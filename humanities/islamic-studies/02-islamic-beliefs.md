# Islamic Beliefs

> [!quote] Quran 2:285
> *The Messenger has believed in what ws sent to him from his Lord, and so have the believers... They believe  in Allah, his angels, his books and his Messengers...*

## Tawhid

Tawhid -> Belief in the Oneness of God
### Types

+ Rububiyah (Lorship) -> He alone is the creator and lord of universe.
+ Uluhiyyah (Worship) -> He alone is the one deserved to be worshipped.
+ Asma Was-Sifaat (Names and Attributes) -> He alone is the keeper of his qualities.

Shirk -> Association of partners with Allah

## Angels

Divine creations of Allah, created from light.

> [!quote] Sahi Muslim 2996
> The angels were created from light...
> 

Primarily worship Allah and follow all of His orders without question. They perform their duties with dedication.

> [!quote] Quran 66:6
> *They do not disobey Allah in what He commands them but do what they are commanded.*

Their number is vast and only God knows about it.

### The Archangels

1. *Jibra'il* -> The angel of revelation
2. *Mikail* -> The angel of sustenance and forces of nature
3. *Israfil* -> The angel of the end of the world and Judgement Day
4. *Izrail* -> The angel of death

## Holy Books

4 divine books sent to mankind for their guidance.
### Tawrah
+ On Prophet Musa AS
+ Provided a comprehensive legal and moral code and it emphasized justice, accountability and social reform.
### Zabur
+ On Prophet Dawud AS
+ Focuses on praise, worship and spiritual wisdom
### Injeel
+ On Prophet Isa AS
+ Emphasized on monotheism and guidance
+ Is different from the four Gospels (Matthew, Mark, Luke, John)
### Quran
+ On Prophet Muhammad SAW
+ Comprehensize manual for life
