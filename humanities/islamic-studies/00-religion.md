# Religion
`Religion is a range/collection of social-cultural systems which include behaviours, ethics, morals, worldviews and texts that relate humanity to supernatural and spiritual elements`

- Over 10,000 distint worldwide religions.
- Top 4 religions in terms of popularity:
	1. Christianity
	2. Islam
	3. Hinduism
	4. Buddhism

- Religion derived from:
	- Latin -> *religionem*: Reverence for the God
	- Frence -> *religion*: Respect for the sacred

> reverence: deep feeling of admiration

## Beliefs

| Belief System   | Definition                                                                                                        | Core Premise                                                                                                    | Example / Representative Figure                                                                              |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Monotheism**  | Belief in the existence of exactly **one** supreme God who created rules and the universe.                        | God is a single, distinct deity who is typically personal, all-powerful, and involved in creation.              | **Islam, Judaism, Christianity.** (e.g., belief in Allah or Yahweh).                                         |
| **Polytheism**  | Belief in **multiple** gods or deities, each often responsible for specific aspects of nature or life.            | The universe is governed by a pantheon of distinct gods with varying powers and roles.                          | **Ancient Greek Mythology** (Zeus, Poseidon, Athena) and **Ancient Egyptian Religion** (Ra, Osiris, Anubis). |
| **Pantheism**   | Belief that God and the universe are identical—**God is everything, and everything is God.**                      | There is no distinct, personal creator separate from reality; the physical cosmos itself is divine.             | **Baruch Spinoza's philosophy** or **Taoism** (where the universe and the *Tao* are inseparable).            |
| **Deism**       | Belief in a supreme creator who designed and set the universe in motion, but **does not intervene** in it.        | God created natural laws and then stepped back; there are no miracles, divine revelations, or answered prayers. | **Voltaire, Thomas Jefferson, and Isaac Newton** held deistic views.                                         |
| **Agnosticism** | The position that the existence of God or the ultimate nature of reality is **unknown or inherently unknowable**. | Claims about the existence or non-existence of God cannot be proven with current human knowledge or logic.      | **Thomas Henry Huxley** (who coined the term) or **Bertrand Russell**.                                       |
| **Atheism**     | The **absence of belief** in the existence of any deities, or the active conviction that no gods exist.           | Rejects theistic claims due to a lack of empirical evidence or logical proof for divine entities.               | **Richard Dawkins, Christopher Hitchens**, or classical secular humanism.                                    |

### Semetic and Non-Semetic Religions
- Semetic religions, also called Abrahamic Religions are monotheistic religions originating from the Far East in Semitic speaking people.
	- Judaism
	- Christianity
	- Islam
- Non Semetic religions originate outside of the Far East.
	- Hinduism
	- Buddhism
	- Sikhism
	- Taoism
