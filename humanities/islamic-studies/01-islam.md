# Islam

## Defintion

> A complete code of life revealed by Allah Almighty to His prophets and messengers from time to time to guide His creation/slaves (Mankind and Jinn) for their success in life and afterlife.

### Etymology

Derived from the Arabic word *salama* -> peace and safeness.
The word *islam* itself means submission, obedience and surrender.

## Subject of Islam

The subject of Islam is that Humans and Jinn have been created solely to worship Allah Almighty.

> [!quote] Quran (51:56)
> *I did not create mankind and Jinn except for the purpose that they should worship me.*

## Objective of Islam

To provide complete guidance to mankind and jinn that leads them to success in life and afterlife.

## Fore Face of Islam

Explained by *Hadeeth-e-Jibril*.

1. Islam
2. Iman (Faith)
3. Ishan (Excellence in Worship)

## Finality of Islam

Islam is the final religion of Allah.

> [!quote] Quran 3:19
> *Truly, the religion with Allah is Islam*

> [!quote] Quran 3:85
> *And, whoever seeks a religion other than Islam, It will never be accepted from him, and he, in hereafter, will be among the losers*

> [!quote] Quran 5:3
> *This day I have perfected for you your religion and completed My favour upon you and have approved for you Islam as your religion*


## Structure of Islam 

+ **Foundation**
	1. Tawheed (Oneness of God)
	2. Risalah (Prophethood)
	3. Akirah (Hereafter)
+ **Religious Structure**
	1. Beliefs (Iman)
		1. Oneness of God
		2. Prophethood
		3. Angels
		4. Holy Books
		5. Day of Judgement
		6. Destiny
	2. Practice (Pillars)
		1. Shahadah
		2. Salah (Prayer)
		3. Sawm (Fast)
		4. Zakah
		5. Hajj (Pilgrimage)
	3. Sources of Law
		1. Quran
		2. Sunnah
		3. Ijma (Consensus)
		4. Qiyas (Analogy)
