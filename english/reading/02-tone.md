## Author's Tone
`Author's Attitude or Emotional position on the topic.`

### Revealed by:
- Word choice
- Imagery
- Sentence structure
	- Short
	- Abrupt
	- Flowing
	- Rhetorical
- Emphasis
- Pacing

> Name the tone and point out the language that creates it.

## Tone vs Mood
1. Tone:
	- Entirely the author's attitude.
	- Absolute and is not subjective.
2. Mood:
	- Feeling created or experienced by the reader.
	- Objective and may vary.

## List of tones

| Tone | Category | When to Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Happy / Joyful** | Emotional | Celebrating achievements, wins, or lighthearted positive news. |
| **Sad / Melancholic** | Emotional | Expressing loss, disappointment, failure, or grief. |
| **Energetic / Enthusiastic** | Emotional | Generating excitement, hyping a product launch, or rallying a team. |
| **Angry / Indignant** | Emotional | Calling out unfairness, severe wrongdoing, or injustice. |
| **Fearful / Anxious** | Emotional | Highlighting immediate danger, security risks, or high vulnerability. |
| **Hopeful / Optimistic** | Emotional | Focusing on future solutions, silver linings, and positive potential. |
| **Pessimistic / Gloomy** | Emotional | Expecting the worst possible outcome or focusing heavily on drawbacks. |
| **Friendly / Warm** | Interpersonal | Building rapport, welcoming someone, or sending casual communications. |
| **Sarcastic / Mocking** | Interpersonal | Using irony to ridicule an idea, poke fun, or highlight absurdity. |
| **Sincere / Heartfelt** | Interpersonal | Offering genuine gratitude, formal apologies, or heartfelt condolences. |
| **Humorous / Playful** | Interpersonal | Entertaining an audience with jokes, light banter, or satire. |
| **Sympathetic / Empathetic** | Interpersonal | Validating someone’s distress, comforting them, or showing shared pain. |
| **Condescending / Patronizing** | Interpersonal | Talking down to someone or assuming intellectual superiority. |
| **Hostile / Aggressive** | Interpersonal | Directly confronting, attacking, or showing explicit antagonism. |
| **Calm / Serene** | Attitudinal | De-escalating high-tension conflicts or describing peaceful environments. |
| **Arrogant / Boastful** | Attitudinal | Flexing superiority, bragging about stats, or displaying self-importance. |
| **Nostalgic / Wistful** | Attitudinal | Looking back fondly or yearningly at past memories or eras. |
| **Serious / Solemn** | Attitudinal | Addressing grave crises, legal matters, or formal ceremonies. |
| **Apathetic / Indifferent** | Attitudinal | Showing a complete lack of interest, care, or engagement. |
| **Reverent / Respectful** | Attitudinal | Demonstrating deep honor, awe, or respect toward a topic or figure. |
| **Cynical / Distrustful** | Attitudinal | Assuming motives are driven entirely by selfish greed or dishonesty. |
| **Objective / Neutral** | Professional | Presenting raw facts, technical manuals, or data without personal bias. |
| **Analytical / Academic** | Professional | Dissecting complex issues using logic, structure, and evidence. |
| **Assertive / Persuasive** | Professional | Convincing an audience to take action or arguing a specific thesis. |
| **Instructional / Didactic** | Professional | Writing tutorials, documentation, step-by-step guides, or onboarding docs. |
