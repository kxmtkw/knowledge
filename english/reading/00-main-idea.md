## Main Idea
`It is a statement which describes the whole passage in a single sentence.`

Can be:
- Stated
- Implied (must be infered)

## For Implied Idea
1. What is the topic?
2. What are the details listed?
3. What is the message of the passage?
4. How can I restate the message in a single line?

## Good Main Idea
- Too Narrow: `No`, too specific
- Too Broad: `No`, overgeneralized
- Correct but Focused: `Yes`

> Answer + Evidence is better than just Answer

## Topic vs Main Idea vs Supporting Details
1. Topic:
	- Name of the passage or subject.
	- Can be a word or phrase.
2. Main Idea:
	- A single statement describing the passage.
	- Must be a sentence (non-questionative)
3. Supporting Details:
	- Details, facts and information which supports the main idea.

> [!tip]
> `furthermore` and `moreover` are often signals for major details.
