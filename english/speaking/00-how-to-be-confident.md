
## How to be confident?

- Speak a bit louder. On a scale of 1-10, aim for 5.
- Pause strategically. Do NOT fill pauses with umm's and ahh's. Instead use pauses to emphasize your points.
- Take a superhero stance.
- Practice a lot.
- Find your silent sentence.
