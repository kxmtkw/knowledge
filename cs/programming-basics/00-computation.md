## Computation
`Computation is any well-defined arithmetic or non-arithmetic calculation that takes an input, follows a specific set of rules, and produces an output.`

## Computational Thinking
The following are essential in the world of computational thinking:

1. Collect data
	- Through input devices and other computers.

2. Analyze data
	- Perform analysis in gathered data to produce information.

3. Pattern Recoginition
	- Finding patterns in data.

4. Build Models
	- Building simulations and models that mirror the real world.

5. Develop Algorithms
	- Make algorithms to solve problems

6. Decompose Problems
	- Breakdown large problems into smaller ones.

### Corner-Stones

1. Decomposition
	- The art of breaking down large problems into smaller problems that are more manageable
2. Pattern Recoginition
	- The art of finding patterns in a seemingly random world and using those patterns.
3. Abstraction
	- The art of hiding complex details behind simple interfaces.
4. Algorithms
	- The art of making step by step instructions to solve a problem.
